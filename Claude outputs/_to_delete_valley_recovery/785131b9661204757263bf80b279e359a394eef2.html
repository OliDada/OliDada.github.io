The Valley

Walk out of the forest and down into the valley. Gather what grows wild, sell it at the castle market, and spend your coin as you please.

WASD or arrow keys to walk. Mouse to look. Shift to run. Space to jump. Click or F to swing. Right-click or hold V to block. Q to raise your torch. C to sneak. E to talk, trade or take things. I opens your bag. Hold T to speed up time.
